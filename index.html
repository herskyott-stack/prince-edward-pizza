Main Street's pizza shop

At 130 Picton Main St, Prince Edward Pizza is the County's everyday slice shop: thin crust, generous toppings, and a lunch slice special that locals plan around. Everything on the menu — pizza, wings, burgers, subs, pasta — is ordered the old way, one phone call at a time. This page puts the whole menu where Google can find it, with one tap to call.
